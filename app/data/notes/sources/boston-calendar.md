The Boston Calendar is an aggregator of Boston-area listings. This batch pulled
all 7 page chunks and kept in-window, in-person, public events in the allowed
cities (2026-09-27 to 2026-10-11). Most in-window items are multi-day attractions,
farmers markets, beer gardens, and seasonal farm activities (apple/pumpkin picking,
sunflower blooms) that start on Sep 27 and run past the window; each is recorded on
its first in-window date with the full run noted in the description.

Skipped: listing/guide articles ("55 things to do", "15 things to do", "102 things
to do", beach/foliage/hiking roundups), venue-only pages, and events located outside
the allowed cities (e.g. The Big E in Springfield, Cider Hill Farm in Woburn, Hopkinton
Center for the Arts in Hopkinton, Tower Hill in Needham, Larcom Theatre in Everett).
Times were not shown for most attractions, so startTime was omitted rather than guessed.
